nLearn

Training coordination, end to end. The enquiry, the quotation, the booking, the trainer, the joining instructions, the certificate and the feedback — in one place instead of five spreadsheets and a shared inbox.

For training providers selling to organisations. Multiple legal entities, several sales areas, any currency, and trainers who are contractors rather than staff — the ordinary case here rather than an awkward exception.

What it does →
  • Enquiry capture, by email or from your website
  • Quotations from your own rate card, several options per enquiry
  • Proposal PDFs generated rather than assembled
  • Client accepts by link; the quotation becomes a booking
  • Trainer register, co-delivery, agreed fees per trainer
  • Joining instructions and pre-course questionnaires
  • Certificates, verifiable without a phone call
  • Course and trainer feedback, and a trainer portal
  • Reporting on who to book, what sells, and where the margin goes
