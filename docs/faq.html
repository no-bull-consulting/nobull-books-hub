About no~bull consulting
What is no~bull consulting?

nBooks
What is nBooks?

nBooks is cloud-based accounting software for UK sole traders and micro-businesses, built for HMRC Making Tax Digital compliance. Features include direct MTD VAT submission, professional invoicing with PDF generation and email delivery, bank reconciliation, multi-currency support, Corporation Tax computation (CT600), and Self Assessment (SA103/ITSA). Priced at £15/month with a 30-day free trial.

Is nBooks HMRC MTD compatible?

Yes. nBooks supports direct MTD VAT submission via the HMRC API and is built for MTD compliance from the ground up. Our full HMRC MTD compatibility statement is available on the site.

How much does nBooks cost?

£15 per month on the Standard plan, which includes all features. A 30-day free trial is available with no credit card required. You can cancel at any time.
