How we work

We don’t lock you in.

Most software suppliers keep the source, keep the hosting, and keep you. We hand over the source, document the infrastructure, and give you a licence that survives us going away. It means we have to keep earning the work rather than relying on how painful leaving would be.

A perpetual licence

Irrevocable, and it survives termination for any reason. Not conditional on a subscription.

The source, quarterly

Full commit history, documentation and deployment configuration, held in your systems.

UK hosting, plainly stated

Named provider, named location, and a security appendix that lists what is not done as well as what is. Read it →

The obvious question

“What if you get run over?”

Fair question, and the honest answer is that no~bull consulting is one person. Pretending otherwise would be the first lie in a relationship that depends on not telling any.

So the arrangement is built around it. You hold the licence, the source and the documentation. The infrastructure is documented well enough for another developer to pick it up. Nothing depends on a conversation with us that could not happen.

That is not a substitute for a larger supplier. It is what makes a small one a reasonable risk.
